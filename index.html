third kind press is a fledgling publisher of small art books.

We take seriously human expressions of radical vulnerability, recalcitrance even, and the unnamable ache and abstract intuition that such expressions can manifest. We open spaces for betweenness, reinvention, and community by bringing collections of art from artists across mediums into the material world in the form of printed matter.
